How zoning maps to your deal

Every site check should answer three questions.

Is the use permitted as-of-right?

If yes, you can typically apply for a building permit without planning approval — the fastest path to move-in.

Does it require a minor variance?

Minor variances go to the Committee of Adjustment. Budget 8–14 weeks, neighbour notice, and a planner's fee — plus some risk of refusal.

Is it prohibited or legal non-conforming?

Prohibited means a full zoning by-law amendment (months of process); legal non-conforming use can continue but not expand.

Want the full municipal overlay?

Pro subscribers get property-level zoning overlays on every listing, with direct links to the governing by-law, setback rules, and height/coverage limits.
