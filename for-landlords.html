How it works for landlords

Three feeds. One lease-up engine.

1. Upload your availabilities

Add every building and every unit you have available. We sync with your listings — or take a simple CSV. Each unit shows rent, size, clear height, door count, power, and availability date.

2. See live tenant demand

Every lease-verified tenant on industrio who is actively searching shows up in your feed — filtered to the criteria they've set: size, submarket, rent, clear height, power, parking, and more. You see anonymized profiles unless they open up.

3. Reach out when there's a match

When a tenant's criteria match one of your units, we flag it. You can initiate a direct message through industrio (routed to the tenant inbox) or send a tour invite — one click.

Why landlords join

Faster lease-up. Fewer vacant months.

Real demand, pre-filtered

Stop listening to brokers tell you what tenants "might" want. industrio shows the criteria tenants have put in writing and verified against a real lease. No tire-kickers.

Shorter time-to-lease

Landlords on industrio close matches 31% faster on median than MLS-only listings — because tenants arrive already qualified and already know your building.

Respond to your reviews

Every published tenant review includes a landlord right-of-reply. Put your side on the record; future tenants see both perspectives.

Benchmark your portfolio

Compare your own rent, TMI, free rent, and retention against anonymized submarket medians. See where your portfolio is competitive and where it isn't.

Keep your data private

Your availabilities are visible to verified tenants with matching criteria only. Your rent and concession data is never sold, never syndicated.

No commissions to us

industrio charges a flat subscription — we take zero commission on any deal. Work with brokers, work direct, whatever fits your business.
