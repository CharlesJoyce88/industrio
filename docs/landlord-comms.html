How it works

Three clicks from empty inbox to searchable record.

Connect your mailbox

Authorize Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. We use read-only delegated access — we never send on your behalf.

Tell us your landlord domains

Enter the property manager's email address. Every message to or from that domain is captured automatically.

Topic classification

industrio reads the subject and body, classifies by topic (rent, maintenance, renewal, TMI, insurance), and threads related messages.

Invite your team

Bring in accounting, operations, or counsel. Each gets role-based read access with internal notes separate from the email content.

Internal notes

Attach context to any thread without replying to the landlord. Notes stay inside industrio.

Dispute-ready

Every message, timestamp, attachment, and note is preserved. Export the full correspondence file as a single PDF when you need it.
