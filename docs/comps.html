Methodology

Where every comp comes from.

TRREB MLS closed-deal feed

Every leased and sold listing closed through TRREB MLS is ingested into industrio within four hours. We scrub for duplicates, confirm square footage against the listing, and enrich with landlord ownership data.

Landlord-portfolio mapping

We maintain a mapping of each major landlord to every building they own — including numbered companies and joint-venture structures — so a "follow ACME" alert fires regardless of which legal entity signs the lease.

Off-market channels

Our relationships with small-bay tenant reps and industrial sub-brokers surface deals that never hit MLS. These are tagged separately so you can filter if you prefer MLS-only.

Rent normalization

Every rent is normalized to effective rent across the term — factoring free rent, TI, escalations, and OpEx. A/A+/B/B+ letter grades tell you at a glance where the deal landed vs. the submarket median.

Alert delivery

Email or in-app push within 60 minutes of the deal posting to MLS. Optional daily digest mode if you'd rather batch.
