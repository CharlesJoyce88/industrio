How it works

From blank page to signed lease, in ten steps.

industrio replaces the scattered spreadsheets, cold calls, and broker tours with a single workflow — transparent at every step.

1

Scan the market

Pull every live industrial listing from TRREB MLS and proprietary off-market sources, filtered to spaces under 50,000 SF that match your criteria.

2

Analyze each space

Whitebox status, shipping doors (number and type), office percentage, parking count and ratio, power (amps/volts/phase), clear height — extracted and normalized automatically.

3

Score the landlord

Verified tenant reviews on responsiveness, maintenance quality, operating cost transparency, renewal fairness, and building condition — surfaced before you commit.

4

Evaluate the location

Demographics, industrial labour concentration, transit access, and 10/20/30-minute drive-time analysis against your customers and suppliers.

5

Flag the risks

Ontario well records, historical spills, former industrial uses, zoning compliance, and neighbouring uses — every red flag surfaced up front.

6

Compare renewal vs. relocation

Honest economics: moving costs, downtime, TI, free rent, and effective rent over term — modeled side by side so you can choose on merit.

7

Build a shortlist

A ranked shortlist with reasoning, not a dump of 80 listings. Each candidate carries a tenant-fit score and the data to back it up.

8

AI contacts listing agents

Professional outreach asking for availability, competing offers, floor plans, site plans, updated photos, and tour windows — tracked in one place.

9

AI generates a tour package

Maps, specs, photos, drive-times, environmental flags, landlord scores, and a tailored list of questions to ask at each stop.

10

Optional: connect to a top broker

If you want human help at the finish line, industrio introduces you to a vetted industrial broker — no lock-in, no hidden fees.
