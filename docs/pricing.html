Frequently asked

Straight answers.

Do you take broker commissions?

No. industrio is paid only by its subscribers. When you choose to work with a human broker, that's a separate relationship, not a kickback to us.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel any time. Annual plans are prorated. No hidden fees, ever.

Do you cover markets outside the GTA?

Today we cover the Greater Toronto Area and immediately adjacent submarkets. Expansion to other Canadian industrial markets is on the roadmap.

Is the environmental screening enough for due diligence?

No. industrio surfaces public records for early-stage screening. A qualified environmental consultant should still perform a Phase I ESA before you sign.
