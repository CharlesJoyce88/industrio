Truck compatibility

Older 1980s-era buildings were built for 40' trailers. Push a 53' tandem through and the court jams, the apron grinds, and the door stop lets in snow.

<\!-- ROOF / SLAB -->
▨

Roof & slab load

Want rooftop solar? Cantilever racking 30' high? A 20-ton press? The building doesn't know until you ask. We read the structural reports so you don't have to.

How we score

Three readings, one fit score per building.

Pass

The building meets or exceeds your requirement with no upgrade work. Ready for move-in as-is.

Upgrade

The building can be brought up to spec with a known scope — transformer upgrade, door enlargement, floor overlay — and we estimate the cost.

Hard stop

The deficiency cannot reasonably be cured (structural slab, hydro service ceiling at the pole, court geometry). Move on.
