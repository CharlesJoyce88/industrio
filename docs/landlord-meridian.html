Operational metrics

Pre-negotiate, or have a plan B.

One in four tenants leaves Meridian at renewal. Of those who stay, roughly a third report signing at above-market rents. If you are considering a Meridian building, industrio recommends negotiating renewal economics — rent caps, TMI audit rights, maintenance SLAs — into the initial lease, not in year five.
