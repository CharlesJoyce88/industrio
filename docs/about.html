Our mission

Small-bay tenants deserve big-tenant advantages.

Industrial tenants under 20,000 SF represent a massive share of the economy — manufacturers, 3PLs, distributors, contractors, fabricators — but the market they lease in is built for the large tenants with dedicated tenant reps and analyst teams. industrio exists to close that gap.

The problem

Industrial leasing data exists — MLS comps, landlord histories, environmental records, demographic and labour statistics, zoning by-laws, drive-time models — but it is fragmented across dozens of paid systems, public records portals, and private broker databases. Small tenants, without a retained tenant rep, rarely see it. The information asymmetry gets priced into their rent, renewal terms, and operating costs for years.

The approach

industrio uses modern data engineering and large language models to aggregate, normalize, and interpret the same information a sophisticated tenant rep would use — then hands it to the tenant directly. No broker commissions. No data reseller markups. No gatekeeping.

The outcome

A small-bay tenant can walk into a renewal conversation with the same grounded view of the market that the landlord has — and walk out with a fairer deal. Or, if relocation makes more sense, they can move on the basis of data, not instinct.

What we will not do

We will not take commission from landlords. We will not prioritize listings based on who pays us. We will not publish unverified reviews. We will not sell tenant data. These aren't marketing promises — they are operational constraints built into the product.

Built by people who have signed industrial leases.

And by people who have read too many bad ones. If industrio's mission resonates, we'd love to hear from you — tenant, broker, landlord, or data partner.
