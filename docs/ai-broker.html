AI Broker

Your tenant-first broker, on duty 24/7.

industrio's AI broker does the unglamorous work of industrial leasing — filtering listings, chasing listing agents, gathering plans, and building clean tour packages — so you walk into every tour prepared and impartial.

Filters spaces by your needs

Size, clear height, shipping, office %, parking, budget, submarket, and operational constraints — the AI works from a structured brief, not a vague email.

Contacts listing agents automatically

Professional, concise outreach drafted per listing — identifying you as the tenant and asking only what's needed to move forward.

Confirms availability

Verifies whether the space is still available, whether offers are in, and the current asking terms — before you invest time in a tour.

Requests plans & details

Floor plans, site plans, updated photos, power specs, roof age, and anything else missing from the listing — pulled into one record.

Schedules tours

Coordinates tour windows directly with listing agents, grouping by geography to keep your day efficient.

Generates a tour package

Maps, key specs, photos, drive-time analytics, environmental flags, landlord scores, and a tailored list of questions — per stop.

Tracks communication

Every email, call note, and document lives in one thread per listing — no more digging through inboxes to find who said what.

Tenant-first by design

industrio is not paid by landlords and never shows listings based on commission splits. The AI's only incentive is your shortlist.

Example outreach

Polished, concise, agent-ready.

Every email industrio sends on your behalf is reviewed by you before it goes out — or set to full-auto once you're comfortable. The AI mirrors a professional tenant rep, without the conflicts.

Draft — awaiting your approval
Subject: 123 Example Rd — availability & plans

Hi [Agent name],

I'm reaching out on behalf of a qualified tenant seeking approximately 15,000 SF of industrial space in the GTA West submarket, targeting occupancy Q3 2026.

123 Example Rd looks like a potential fit. Could you confirm:
  • Is the space still available?
  • Are there any offers in play?
  • Could you share floor plans, site plans, and recent photos?
  • When are tour windows available this week or next?

Happy to sign an NDA if required.

Thanks,
industrio, on behalf of [Tenant]
