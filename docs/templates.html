Document library

The document templates your lawyer charges to draft.

Every industrial leasing and purchase document a tenant or buyer typically needs — drafted by Ontario commercial counsel, customizable with AI, and free to download as a starting point. Have your lawyer review the final before signing.

Leasing

Lease negotiation & administration.

From first offer to signed lease, plus everything you need to administer the tenancy.

Lease — most used

Offer to Lease (OTL)

The binding offer that locks in base rent, term, free rent, TI, deposits, and conditions. Ontario-standard, tenant-friendly default language.

Lease

Letter of Intent (LOI)

Non-binding outline of key commercial terms. Useful for early-stage negotiations or multi-party RFPs, without the full offer commitment.

Lease

Industrial Net Lease

Full triple-net lease agreement covering rent, TMI, maintenance, insurance, use, compliance, and surrender — tenant-negotiated defaults.

Renewal

Lease Renewal / Extension

Renewal or extension of an existing lease. Use with current market comp data to negotiate rent reset, TI, and free-rent period.

Sublet

Sublease Agreement

Sublet part or all of your space to a third party — with a back-to-back flow-through to the head lease.

Admin

Estoppel Certificate

Standard tenant estoppel confirming lease terms, rent, deposits, and defaults — usually required for landlord financing or sale.

Admin

Commencement Date Confirmation

Short agreement setting the actual occupancy and rent-commencement dates once fixturing is complete.

Admin

Lease Assignment

Assign your lease to a new tenant (typically on a business sale) with landlord consent and release of guarantor obligations where negotiated.

Purchase & condo

Buying an industrial unit.

From LOI to closing, including condo-specific documents for small-bay strata units.

Purchase — most used

Agreement of Purchase & Sale

OREA-style APS customized for industrial condos and standalone buildings. Includes standard conditions: financing, inspection, environmental, status certificate.

Purchase

Letter of Intent (LOI)

Non-binding intent to purchase with headline price, closing, conditions, and exclusivity — useful before investing in full due diligence.

Condo

Status Certificate Request

Formal request to the condo corporation for the status certificate, including reserve fund study, budget, bylaws, and special-assessment history.

Seller

Vendor Disclosure Statement

For sellers: comprehensive disclosure covering title, encumbrances, tenant schedules, known defects, environmental, and litigation.

Due diligence & operations

Checklists, audits, and condition reports.

The documents that keep landlords honest and surface problems before they become yours.

Due diligence

Phase I ESA Scope of Work

Request-for-proposal scope for a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment, written to Canadian Standards Association (CSA) Z768.

OpEx

TMI / OpEx Audit Request

Formal letter to the landlord exercising your lease's audit right on operating cost reconciliations, including document list.

Tour

Building Condition Checklist

Pre-tour and post-tour checklist: roof, HVAC, floor slab, dock equipment, power, washrooms, lighting, fire suppression.

Compliance

Zoning & Use Verification

Letter to the municipal zoning examiner confirming the intended use is permitted, plus outdoor storage and signage allowances.
