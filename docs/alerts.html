How it works

From listing published to inquiry sent — in under an hour.

1

You set criteria, once.

Size, clear height, shipping, office %, budget, submarkets, and must-haves. Edit anytime.

2

industrio monitors continuously.

TRREB MLS and proprietary off-market sources are re-scanned every 15 minutes.

3

Match detected → you're notified.

Email, SMS, or Slack — whichever you picked. The notification includes a full space summary, landlord score, and environmental flags.

4

One click to inquire.

Approve the AI broker's draft outreach and it goes out to the listing agent immediately. Tour windows usually confirmed inside 24 hours.

5

Track every thread.

All agent communication, documents, and availability updates live under each listing. No inbox chaos.

Be first through the door.

In a market where quality small-bay space gets leased within days, being the first qualified tenant to tour is often the only advantage that matters.
