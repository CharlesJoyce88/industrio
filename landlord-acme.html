Operational metrics

Facts, measured — not testimonials.

ACME's median maintenance acknowledgment is under 4 hours and most non-urgent work is on-site within 2 days. Three-quarters of tenants renew at market rent or better, with roughly 1 in 5 renewals coming in above market — typically in Class A buildings with recent roof or HVAC upgrades.
